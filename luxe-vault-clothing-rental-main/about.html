About Luxe Vault

Rent Luxury. Define Elegance.

Our Story

Luxe Vault began with a simple idea: nobody should have to spend a fortune on an outfit they will wear once. Weddings, galas, college farewells and festive nights all call for something special, yet designer wear is expensive and usually ends up unused in a wardrobe.

We keep a curated vault of lehengas, sherwanis, tuxedos, gowns and party wear that you can rent for the days you need it. Every garment is professionally cleaned, steam-pressed and delivered to your door.
